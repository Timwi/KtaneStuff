[[Barbora Krejčíková]] defeated [[Jasmine Paolini]] in the final, 6–2, 2–6, 6–4 to win the ladies' singles tennis title at the [[2024 Wimbledon Championships]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.eurosport.com/tennis/wimbledon-women/2024/barbora-krejcikova-crowned-wimbledon-champion-for-first-time-as-czech-downs-jasmine-paolini-in-three-sets_sto20017662/story.shtml|title=Barbora Krejcikova crowned Wimbledon champion for first time|publisher=Eurosport|access-date=13 July 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://news.sky.com/story/krejcikova-crowned-wimbledon-womens-singles-champion-13177465|title=Krejcikova crowned Wimbledon women's singles champion|publisher=Sky News|access-date=13 July 2024}}</ref> It was her first [[Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] singles title and second [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] singles title overall.

[[Markéta Vondroušová]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite news |last1=Futterman |first1=Matthew |title=Marketa Vondrousova wins Wimbledon and her first Grand Slam title |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2023/07/15/sports/tennis/wimbledon-championship-jabeur-vondrousova.html |access-date=24 June 2024 |work=The New York Times |date=15 July 2023}}</ref> but lost in the first round to [[Jéssica Bouzas Maneiro]]. This marked only the second time in the [[Open Era]] that the defending Wimbledon champion lost in the first round, after [[Steffi Graf]] lost to [[Lori McNeil]] 30 years ago, in [[1994 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|1994]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Fendrich |first1=Howard |title=Marketa Vondrousova is the first defending women's Wimbledon champ out in the first round since 1994 |url=https://apnews.com/article/wimbledon-7-2-2024-results-a4378df0598b6b1419141e61f4bbeec2 |website=AP News |access-date=2 July 2024 |date=2 July 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240709030011/https://apnews.com/article/wimbledon-7-2-2024-results-a4378df0598b6b1419141e61f4bbeec2?__cf_chl_rt_tk=5Ug.FYGl6J6BUFGxeFzZ_mZClMaM7QfNXzfEPBzrd1Y-1720494011-0.0.1.1-3626 |archive-date=9 July 2024}}</ref> 

Paolini was the first Italian woman in the Open Era to reach the Wimbledon semifinals and final.<ref>{{cite tweet|url=https://x.com/Wimbledon/status/1810738890376106486|number=1810738890376106486|user=Wimbledon|title=The first Italian woman in the Open Era to make the semi-finals at #Wimbledon|date=9 July 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4054759/takeaways-paolini-reaches-wimbledon-semifinals-makes-italian-history|title=Takeaways: Paolini reaches Wimbledon semifinals, makes Italian history|website=WTAtennis.com|date=9 July 2024}}</ref> She became the first woman to reach back-to-back [[French Open]] and Wimbledon finals since [[Serena Williams]] in 2016, and the first Italian woman in the Open Era to reach the final of two different majors.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4056608/paolini-vs-krejcikova-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-wimbledon-final|title=Paolini vs. Krejcikova: What you need to know about the Wimbledon final|website=WTAtennis.com|date=11 July 2024}}</ref> The semifinal match between Paolini and [[Donna Vekić]] was the longest women's semifinal in Wimbledon history, at two hours and 51 minutes.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4056243/paolini-outlasts-vekic-in-record-breaking-wimbledon-semifinal|title=Paolini outlasts Vekic in record-breaking Wimbledon semifinal|website=WTAtennis.com|date=11 July 2024}}</ref>

This marked the final major appearance of [[2018 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2018]] champion, three-time major champion and former world No. 1 [[Angelique Kerber]]. She lost in the first round to [[Yulia Putintseva]].
