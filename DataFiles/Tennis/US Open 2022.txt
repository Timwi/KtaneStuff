[[Carlos Alcaraz]] defeated [[Casper Ruud]] in the final, 6–4, 2–6, 7–6<sup>(7–1)</sup>, 6–3 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2022 US Open (tennis)|2022 US Open]]. It was his first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title. Alcaraz claimed the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]] for the first time with the win;<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-09-11/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_casper_ruud_for_2022_us_open_title_world_no_1_ranking.html|title=Carlos Alcaraz defeats Casper Ruud for 2022 US Open title, world No. 1 ranking|first=Arthur|last=Kapetanakis|date=11 September 2022|website=[[US Open (tennis)|US Open]]|access-date=12 September 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220912091653/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-09-11/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_casper_ruud_for_2022_us_open_title_world_no_1_ranking.html|archive-date=12 September 2022}}</ref> Ruud, [[Rafael Nadal]], [[Daniil Medvedev]], and [[Stefanos Tsitsipas]] were also in contention for the top position.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/nadal-medvedev-alcaraz-us-open-2022-world-no-1-scenarios|title=Rafael Nadal, Daniil Medvedev Lead Five-Way Battle For No. 1 At US Open|date=23 August 2022|website=ATP Tour|access-date=12 September 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://archive.today/20220912013713/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/nadal-medvedev-alcaraz-us-open-2022-world-no-1-scenarios|archive-date=12 September 2022}}</ref> He [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Won a title after saving match points|saved a match point]] en route to the title, in the quarterfinals against [[Jannik Sinner]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/alcaraz-sinner-us-open-2022-qf|title=Carlos Alcaraz Saves MP, Outlasts Jannik Sinner In Historic US Open QF Thriller|date=8 September 2022|website=ATP Tour|access-date=12 September 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220912010205/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/alcaraz-sinner-us-open-2022-qf|archive-date=12 September 2022}}</ref> Alcaraz was the youngest men's major champion since Nadal at the [[2005 French Open – Men's singles|2005 French Open]], the youngest [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] champion since [[Pete Sampras]] in [[1990 US Open – Men's singles|1990]], the first man born in the 2000s to win a major singles title, and the youngest man to be ranked world No. 1, surpassing [[Lleyton Hewitt]]'s record.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/34574972/carlos-alcaraz-19-wins-us-open-become-youngest-world-no-1-men-tennis-history|title=Carlos Alcaraz, 19, wins US Open to become youngest world No. 1 in men's tennis history|date=12 September 2022|website=ESPN.com|access-date=12 September 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/34559302/carlos-alcaraz-races-us-open-final-5-set-win-frances-tiafoe|title=Carlos Alcaraz to play for US Open title, shot at No. 1 ranking after 5-set win over Frances Tiafoe|date=10 September 2022|website=ESPN.com|access-date=10 September 2022}}</ref> At 23 hours and 39 minutes of play duration across his seven matches, Alcaraz spent the longest time on court in major history (a record later surpassed by Medvedev at the [[2024 Australian Open – Men's singles|2024 Australian Open]]).<ref>{{Cite news |last=Carayol |first=Tumaini |date=2024-01-28 |title=Jannik Sinner sinks Daniil Medvedev to win first slam title at Australian Open |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/jan/28/jannik-sinner-daniil-medvedev-australian-open-final-mens-singles-tennis |access-date=2024-02-09 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

Medvedev was the defending champion,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2021-09-12/daniil_medvedev_ends_novak_djokovics_slam_hopes_with_inspired_us_open_triumph.html|title=Daniil Medvedev ends Novak Djokovic's Slam hopes with inspired US Open triumph|first=Chris|last=Oddo|date=12 September 2021|website=US Open|access-date=20 July 2022}}</ref> but lost in the fourth round to [[Nick Kyrgios]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/acb1b790a91a4488e56873633958e6c1|title=Kyrgios beats 2021 champ Medvedev, discusses mental state|first=Howard|last=Fendrich|date=5 September 2022|website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=5 September 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220905081029/https://apnews.com/article/novak-djokovic-rafael-nadal-us-open-tennis-championships-sports-asia-acb1b790a91a4488e56873633958e6c1|archive-date=5 September 2022}}</ref> Medvedev was the first man outside the [[Big Three (tennis)#Big Four era|Big Four]] to be the top seed at a major since [[Andy Roddick]] at the [[2004 Australian Open – Men's singles|2004 Australian Open]].<ref>{{cite tweet|author=US Open Tennis|author-link=US Open (tennis)|user=usopen|number=1562843432455774210|title=Changing of the guard?|date=25 August 2022|access-date=25 August 2022}}</ref>

Nadal was vying for a [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|record-extending]] 23rd major singles title, but lost in the fourth round to [[Frances Tiafoe]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/05/sports/tennis/nadal-tiafoe-us-open.html|url-access=limited|title=Rafael Nadal Loses His Serve and His Way at the U.S. Open|last=Clarey|first=Christopher|author-link=Christopher Clarey|date=5 September 2022|website=[[The New York Times]]|access-date=6 September 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220906134740/https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/05/sports/tennis/nadal-tiafoe-us-open.html|archive-date=6 September 2022}}</ref> Alongside Medvedev's loss at the same stage, that marked the first US Open since [[2000 US Open – Men's singles|2000]] and the first major since the [[2017 Australian Open – Men's singles|2017 Australian Open]] with neither of the top two seeded men reaching the quarterfinals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/f9bd0cc2d7b6f58166f592202f01a4db|title=Tiafoe ends Nadal's 22-match Slam streak in US Open 4R|first=Howard|last=Fendrich|date=6 September 2022|website=Associated Press|access-date=8 September 2022}}</ref> This was also the four-time champion's final appearance at the US Open. Three-time champion [[Novak Djokovic]] withdrew prior to the tournament as he could not travel to the United States, due to not having complied with the federal government's [[Visa policy of the United States#Restricted entry or visa issuance|vaccination policy for non-US citizens]] against [[COVID-19]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/djokovics-withdraws-from-the-us-open-and-his-grand-slam-season-is-over-619520.html|title=Djokovic withdraws from the US Open and his Grand Slam season is over|first=Chris|last=Oddo|date=25 August 2022|website=Tennis Majors|access-date=26 August 2022}}</ref>

This marked the third consecutive US Open where a player claimed his first major title, after [[Dominic Thiem]] in [[2020 US Open – Men's singles|2020]] and Medvedev in [[2021 US Open – Men's singles|2021]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.itftennis.com/media/8254/2022-wimbledon-mens-tournament-preview-third-edition.pdf|title=2022 Wimbledon - men's tournament preview|format=PDF|page=17|publisher=[[International Tennis Federation]]|access-date=6 September 2022}}</ref> The quarterfinal line-up guaranteed a first-time [[List of Grand Slam men's singles champions|major champion]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.eurosport.com/tennis/us-open/2022/us-open-2022-mens-quarter-final-preview-with-a-maiden-grand-slam-winner-guaranteed-and-nick-kyrgios-_sto9128182/story.shtml|title=New York, new champion: who will clinch their maiden Grand Slam crown?|first=Michael|last=Hincks|date=6 September 2022|website=[[Eurosport]]|access-date=6 September 2022}}</ref> while the semifinal line-up marked the first time all four players made their US Open semifinal debut since the inaugural edition in [[1881 U.S. National Championships – Singles|1881]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/34557746/casper-ruud-us-open-final-first-grand-slam-title-no-1-ranking-reach|title=Casper Ruud into US Open final as first Grand Slam title, No. 1 ranking within reach|date=10 September 2022|website=ESPN.com|access-date=10 September 2022}}</ref> Tiafoe was the first American man to reach the semifinals since Roddick in [[2006 US Open – Men's singles|2006]], the first [[African American]] man to do so since [[Arthur Ashe]] in [[1972 US Open – Men's singles|1972]], and the first African American man to reach any major semifinal since [[MaliVai Washington]] in [[1996 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|1996]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.marca.com/en/tennis/us-open/2022/09/08/63195e9022601d056a8b4591.html|title=Frances Tiafoe rocked Arthur Ashe Stadium as he equaled the milestone achieved 50 years ago by Arthur Ashe himself|date=7 September 2022|website=[[Marca (newspaper)|marca.com]]|access-date=8 September 2022}}</ref> Sinner was the youngest man to reach the [[Open Era tennis records – Men's singles#At each Grand Slam tournament totals|quarterfinals at all four majors]] since Djokovic in [[2008 Australian Open – Men's singles|2008]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/us-open-sinner-books-spot-in-quarter-finals-623297.html|title=Sinner: youngest player to reach quarter-finals of all 4 Grand Slam events since Djokovic|date=6 September 2022|website=tennismajors.com|access-date=8 September 2022}}</ref>

This was the first edition of US Open to feature a [[Tennis scoring system#Scoring a tiebreak game|10-point tie-break]], when the score reaches six games all in the deciding set.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-03-16/us_open_to_join_all_grand_slams_in_playing_10point_final_set_tiebreak.html|title=US Open to join all Grand Slams in playing 10-point final set tiebreak|date=16 March 2022|website=US Open|access-date=30 August 2022|url-status=live|archive-url=https://archive.today/20220901221942/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-02-28/2022-02-28_2022-02-28_reigning_us_open_champion_daniil_medvedev_breaks_new_ground_as_world_no_1.html|archive-date=1 September 2022}}</ref> [[Pedro Cachin]] defeated [[Aljaž Bedene]] in the first round in the first main-draw 10-point tie-break at the US Open.
