[[Rafael Nadal]] defeated [[Casper Ruud]] in the final, 6–3, 6–3, 6–0 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2022 French Open]]. It was his record-extending 14th [[French Open]] title and [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|record-extending]] 22nd and last [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Abulleil|first=Reem|date=5 June 2022|title=It's Grand Slam No.22 for Rafa|url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2022-nadal-ruud-final-report|url-status=live|archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220605160615/https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2022-nadal-ruud-final-report|archive-date=5 Slam|url=https://ausopen.com/articles/news/14th-roland-garros-title-nadal-halfway-grand-slam|url-status=live|archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220619100635/https://ausopen.com/articles/news/14th-roland-garros-title-nadal-halfway-grand-slam|archive-date=19 June 2022|access-date=19 June 2022|website=[[Australian Open]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Clarey|first=Christopher|date=6 June 2022|title=Nadal Has a Chance at Winning All 4 Grand Slams, if His Foot Cooperates|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2022/06/06/sports/tennis/rafael-nadal-injury.html|url-status=live|archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220613110633/https://www.nytimes.com/2022/06/06/sports/tennis/rafael-nadal-injury.html|archive-date=13 June 2022|access-date=13 June 2022|website=[[The New York Times]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Clarey|first=Christopher|date=17 June 2022|title=Rafael Nadal, Chasing Grand Slam, Will Head to June 2022|access-date=22 June 2022|website=[[The New York Times]]}}</ref> It was also his 92nd and final [[ATP Tour]]-level singles title. Nadal was the third man to defeat four top-10 players en route to a major title since the introduction of [[ATP rankings]] in 1973 (after [[Mats Wilander]] at the [[1982 French Open – Men's singles|1982 French Open]] and [[Roger Federer]] at the [[2017 Australian Open – Men's singles|2017 Australian Open]]).<ref>{{Cite web|date=5 June 2022|first=Sam|last=Jacot|title=Rafael Nadal Defeats Casper Ruud For 14th Roland Garros Title|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/nadal-ruud-roland-garros-2022-final-sunday|website=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]|access-date=22 June 2022}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=7 June 2022|title=Joy after pain: On Nadal and Swiatek win at French Open|url=https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/joy-after-pain/article65500684.ece|url-status=live|archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220613150627/https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/joy-after-pain/article65500684.ece|archive-date=13 June 2022|access-date=13 June 2022|website=[[The Hindu]]}}</ref> Ruud was the first Norwegian man to reach a major quarterfinal and beyond (surpassing his father [[Christian Ruud|Christian]]), and the first Scandinavian man to do so since [[Robin Söderling]] in [[2010 French Open – Men's singles|2010]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=3 June 2022|title=Ruud Rises Above Cilic Into First Slam Final|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/ruud-cilic-roland-garros-2022-sf|url-status=live|archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220613130618/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/ruud-cilic-roland-garros-2022-sf|archive-date=13 June 2022|website=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]|access-date=4 June 2022}}</ref>

[[Novak Djokovic]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Abulleil |first=Reem |date=13 June 2021 |title=History for Djokovic |url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2021-djokovic-tsitsipas-final-report |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220214135953/https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2021-djokovic-tsitsipas-final-report |archive-date=14 February 2022 |access-date=12 April 2022 }}</ref> but lost in the quarterfinals to Nadal.<ref>{{Cite web |date=31 May 2022 |title=Rafael Nadal Downs Novak Djokovic In Classic Roland Garros Quarter-final |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-nadal-roland-garros-2022-qf |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220601062616/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-nadal-roland-garros-2022-qf |archive-date=1 June 2022 |access-date=31 May 2022 |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]] }}</ref> Their quarterfinal match was their record-extending [[Djokovic–Nadal rivalry|59th encounter]], and their tenth encounter at the French Open, an [[Open Era]] record for two players in one tournament.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Fendrich |first=Howard |date=1 June 2022 |title=Nadal tops Djokovic in quarterfinal thriller at French Open |work=[[Associated Press]] |url=https://apnews.com/article/french-open-rafael-nadal-novak-djokovic-sports-sloane-stephens-8983708f1cc21f6163479ccd270c93aa |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220601070816/https://apnews.com/article/french-open-rafael-nadal-novak-djokovic-sports-sloane-stephens-8983708f1cc21f6163479ccd270c93aa |archive-date=1 June 2022 |access-date=1 June 2022 }}</ref>

Djokovic, [[Daniil Medvedev]] and [[Alexander Zverev]] were in contention for the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]]. Following Djokovic's quarterfinal loss and Zverev's semifinal retirement, Djokovic retained the top position at the end of the tournament. One week later, when points from the previous year's tournament were dropped,<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 May 2022 |title=How Medvedev's Roland Garros Loss Changed The Battle For World No. 1 |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/battle-for-no-1-roland-garros-2022-post-r4-update |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220531091442/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/battle-for-no-1-roland-garros-2022-post-r4-update |archive-date=31 May 2022 |access-date=1 June 2022 |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]] }}</ref> Medvedev reclaimed the top ranking, while Zverev reached world No. 2, ending the [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]]'s streak in the top two spots since 10 November 2003. Additionally, as Federer did not enter the tournament, he fell out of the top 50 in the ATP rankings for the first time since 5 June 2000.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Ramchandani|first=Haresh|date=6 June 2022|title=Djokovic remains atop ATP rankings but Medvedev to become No 1 next week; Nadal up to No 4|url=https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/djokovic-remains-atop-atp-rankings-but-medvedev-to-become-no-1-next-week-nadal-up-to-no-4-605090.html|access-date=9 June 2022}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Ramchandani|first=Haresh|date=6 April 2022|title=Federer drops out of Top 40 for the first time since June 2000|url=https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/federer-drops-out-of-top-40-for-the-first-time-since-june-2000-594966.html|access-date=9 June 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbssports.com/tennis/news/daniil-medvedev-takes-no-1-world-ranking-ending-18-year-streak-of-tennis-big-three-dominating-top-spots/|title=Daniil Medvedev takes No. 1 world ranking, ending 18-year streak of tennis' 'Big Three ' dominating top spots|work=[[CBS Sports]]|last=Gonzalez|first=Isabel|date=13 June 2022|access-date=14 June 2022}}</ref>

[[Stefanos Tsitsipas]]' fourth-round loss guaranteed a first-time French Open finalist from the bottom half of the draw, with Ruud emerging as that finalist.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/61636645|title=French Open 2022: Daniil Medvedev and Stefanos Tsitsipas suffer shock exits at Roland Garros|date=30 May 2022|first=Michael|last=Beardmore|access-date=3 June 2022|website=[[BBC]]}}</ref> [[Holger Rune]] was the first Danish man to reach a major singles quarterfinal in the Open Era.<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 May 2022 |title=Holger Rune Shocks Stefanos Tsitsipas To Continue Dream Run |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/tsitsipas-rune-roland-garros-2022-monday |access-date=31 May 2022 |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref> This tournament marked the first time since the [[2002 Australian Open – Men's singles|2002 Australian Open]] that [[Feliciano López]] failed to qualify for the main draw of a major, ending his record streak of 79 [[All-time tennis records – Men's singles#Consecutive totals|consecutive major appearances]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/roland-garros-2022-tuesday-qualifying|title=Feliciano Lopez Falls In Roland Garros Qualifying As Grand Slam Streak Ends|publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]|date=17 May 2022|access-date=18 May 2022}}</ref> This marked the final professional appearance of former world No. 5 and [[2008 Australian Open – Men's singles|2008 Australian Open]] runner-up [[Jo-Wilfried Tsonga]], who lost in the first round to Ruud.<ref>{{cite web |last=Eichenholz |first=Andrew |date=24 May 2022 |title=Merci, Jo: The Joie De Vivre Of Jo-Wilfried Tsonga |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/tsonga-roland-garros-2022-retirement-tribute |access-date=1 June 2022 |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref>

This was the first edition of the French Open to feature a [[Glossary of tennis terms#T|tiebreak]] (10-point) in the fifth set when the match reached 6 games all.<ref>{{Cite web|title=The End of the Endless Final Set: Grand Slams Adopt Same Tiebreaker|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2022/03/17/sports/tennis/grand-slams-tiebreaker.html|first=Christopher|last=Clarey|date=17 March 2022|access-date=24 May 2022|work=[[The New York Times]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220509172915/https://www.nytimes.com/2022/03/17/sports/tennis/grand-slams-tiebreaker.html|archive-date=9 May 2022}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.reuters.com/lifestyle/sports/slams-trial-10-point-tiebreak-final-set-starting-with-roland-garros-2022-03-16/|title=Slams to trial 10-point tiebreak in final set starting with Roland Garros|date=16 March 2022|access-date=3 June 2022|website=[[Reuters]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2022-03-16/grand_slam_tournaments_jointly_announce_10point_final_set_tiebreak_at_six_games_all.html|title=Grand Slam Tournaments Jointly Announce 10-Point Final Set Tie-Break at Six Games All|date=16 March 2022|access-date=3 June 2022|website=[[The Championships, Wimbledon]]}}</ref> [[Camilo Ugo Carabelli]] and [[Aslan Karatsev]] became the first players to contest this tiebreak in the main draw in their first-round match, with Ugo Carabelli emerging victorious.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Zugasti |first=Marcos |date=23 May 2022 |title=Camilo Ugo Carabelli: The Pirate Looking To Plunder In Paris |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/ugo-carabelli-roland-garros-2022-feature |access-date=1 June 2022 |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref>

== Seeded players ==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on ATP rankings as of 16 May 2022. Rankings and points before are as of 23 May 2022.

Because the 2022 tournament takes place one week earlier than in 2021, points from the 2020 and 2021 tournaments will not be dropped until 13 June 2022, one week after the end of the 2022 tournament. Those points are accordingly not reflected in the table below. Instead of points from the 2020 and 2021 tournaments, players will be dropping either (a) points from tournaments held during the week of 24 May 2021 ([[2021 Belgrade Open – Singles|Belgrade 2]] and [[2021 Emilia-Romagna Open – Men's singles|Parma]]) or (b) their 19th best result.

Note that this is a different rankings adjustment system than the one that the WTA is using for the women's tournament.
