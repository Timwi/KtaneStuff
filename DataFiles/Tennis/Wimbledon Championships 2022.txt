Three-time defending champion [[Novak Djokovic]] defeated [[Nick Kyrgios]] in the 3-hour final, 4–6, 6–3, 6–4, 7–6<sup>(7–3)</sup> to win the gentlemen's singles tennis title at the [[2022 Wimbledon Championships]]. It was his seventh [[Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] title and 21st [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] singles title overall.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2022-07-10/seventh_title_for_unbeatable_djokovic.html|title=Seventh Wimbledon title for unbeatable Djokovic|first=Mark|last=Hodgkinson|date=10 July 2022|website=Wimbledon|access-date=11 July 2022}}</ref> Djokovic was the fifth man in the [[Open Era]] to win [[All-time tennis records – Men's singles#Consecutive titles per Grand Slam tournament|four consecutive titles]] at one major (joining [[Björn Borg]], [[Pete Sampras]], [[Roger Federer]], and [[Rafael Nadal]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ausopen.com/articles/news/djokovics-longevity-could-prove-greatest-triumph-all|title=Djokovic's longevity could prove greatest triumph of all|first=Matt|last=Trollope|date=11 July 2022|access-date=2 September 2022|website=ausopen.com|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220902123336/https://ausopen.com/articles/news/djokovics-longevity-could-prove-greatest-triumph-all|archive-date=2 September 2022}}</ref> By reaching his 32nd men's singles major final, he surpassed Federer's [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|all-time record]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/wimbledon-rafael-nadal-novak-djokovic-sports-england-e2527fbbaf7315472a368e09bc96e04e|title=Djokovic moves into 8th Wimbledon final; will face Kyrgios|first=Howard|last=Fendrich|date=8 July 2022|website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=8 July 2022}}</ref> Djokovic became the first player (male or female) to win [[All-time tennis records – Men's singles#Match wins per Grand Slam tournament|80 matches]]<!--[[All-time tennis records – Women's singles#Match wins per tournament]]--> at all four majors with his first-round win.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.timesnownews.com/sports/tennis/wimbledon-novak-djokovic-creates-history-becomes-first-male-player-to-win-80-matches-in-all-4-grand-slams-article-92498316|title=Wimbledon: Novak Djokovic creates history, becomes first male player to win 80 matches in all 4 Grand Slams|date=27 June 2022|website=[[Times Now]]|access-date=9 July 2022}}</ref> Because the [[Association of Tennis Professionals]] (ATP) decided not to award ranking points in response to Wimbledon's [[Wimbledon ban on Russian and Belarusian players|banning of Russian and Belarusian players]], Djokovic dropped out of the top five in the [[ATP rankings]] after winning the tournament.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/tennis/how-the-war-in-russia-impacts-professional-tennis-7934292/|title=Why Novak Djokovic will drop 2,000 ranking points despite winning Wimbledon|first=Namit|last=Kumar|website=The Indian Express|date=24 May 2022 |access-date=11 July 2022}}</ref>

Nadal was attempting to complete the third component of a prospective [[List of Grand Slam men's singles champions#Grand Slam achievements|Grand Slam]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/adamzagoria/2022/07/07/rafael-nadal-has-an-abdominal-tear-but-wants-to-play-wimbledon-semifinal-against-nick-kyrgios-report/|url-access=limited|title=Rafael Nadal Withdraws From Wimbledon Semifinal As Grand Slam Bid Comes To An End|first=Adam|last=Zagoria|date=7 July 2022|access-date=2 September 2022|website=forbes.com}}</ref> but withdrew before the semifinals due to a torn abdominal muscle.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2022-07-07/2022-07-07_nadal_pulls_out_of_wimbledon_through_injury.html|title=Nadal pulls out of Wimbledon through injury|first=Alex|last=Sharp|date=7 July 2022|website=Wimbledon|access-date=7 July 2022}}</ref> It was the first time a man withdrew from a Wimbledon semifinal or final since [[Frank Shields]] in [[1931 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|1931]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/wimbledon-rafael-nadal-novak-djokovic-sports-tennis-228fd350359256e0fa38a9db7073b746|title=Injured Nadal out of Wimbledon; Kyrgios advances to final|first=Howard|last=Fendrich|date=7 July 2022|website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=8 July 2022}}</ref> This was the two-time champion's final appearance at Wimbledon.

This was the first edition of Wimbledon to feature a champions{{efn-lr|also referred to as [[Glossary of tennis terms#S|super tie-break]]}} [[Tennis scoring system#Scoring a tiebreak game|tiebreak]] (10-point tiebreak), when the score reaches six games all in the fifth set.<ref>{{Cite 10-Point Final Set Tie-Break at Six Games All|date=16 March 2022|access-date=3 June 2022|website=wimbledon.com}}</ref>{{efn-lr|The previous tie-break rule was a 7-point tie-break, when the score reached twelve games all in the fifth set.}} [[Alejandro Davidovich Fokina]] defeated [[Hubert Hurkacz]] in the first round in the first main-draw 10-point tiebreak at Wimbledon.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/tennis/wimbledon-2022/alejandro-davidovich-fokina-wins-wimbledons-first-10-point-final-set-tiebreak/articleshow/92499356.cms|title=Alejandro where both the reigning world No. 1 and No. 2 ([[Daniil Medvedev]] and [[Alexander Zverev]], respectively) did not compete; Medvedev was prohibited from playing due to his Russian nationality, while Zverev was recovering from an ankle injury sustained at the [[2022 French Open – Men's singles|French Open]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=15 June 2022|title=Alexander Zverev Wimbledon withdrawal results in 2022|website=express.co.uk|publisher=[[Daily Express]]}}</ref> This marked the first edition of Wimbledon since [[1998 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|1998]] not to feature record eight-time web|url=https://www.express.co.uk/sport/tennis/1630844/Roger-Federer-ATP-rankings-Wimbledon-Rafa-Nadal-Novak-Djokovic-tennis-news|title=Roger Federer to fall off ATP rankings after Wimbledon causing Open]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com.au/news/2022/07/08/kyrgios-advances-to-wimbledon-final-as-nadal-withdraws|title=Kyrgios advances to Wimbledon final, as Nadal withdraws|first=Leigh|last=Rogers|date=8 July 2022|access-date=2 September 2022|website=tennis.com.au}}</ref> [[Feliciano López]] made his 81st (and final) main-draw major appearance, tying Federer's records#Aces|record of 13,728 career aces]] in his third-round match against [[Jannik Sinner]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/isner-aces-record-2022|title=John Isner Sets New ==Seeded players==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on ATP rankings as of 20 June 2022. Rankings and points are as before 27 June 2022.

As a result of special ranking adjustment rules due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]], players are defending the higher of (i) their points from the [[2021 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2021 tournament]] or (ii) the remaining 50% of their points from the [[2019 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2019 tournament]]. Those points were not mandatory and are included in the table below only if they counted towards the player's ranking as of 27 June 2022. Note that this is a different rankings adjustment system than the one that the WTA is using for the women's tournament.

The ATP is removing ranking points from the 2022 tournament as a result of the [[All England Club]]'s decision to [[Wimbledon ban on Russian and Belarusian players|ban Russian and Belarusian players]] from the tournament.<ref>{{Cite web |date=20 May 2022 |title=ATP Statement On Removal Of Ranking Points At 2022 Wimbledon {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/atp-statement-removal-of-ranking-points-wimbledon-2022 |access-date= |website=ATP Tour |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref> Points dropping from the 2021 or 2019 tournaments will accordingly be replaced by the player's next best result, regardless of his performance at Wimbledon in 2022.<ref>{{Cite web |date=20 May 2022 |title=FAQ: Removal Of ATP Rankings Points From 2022 Wimbledon {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/atp-wimbledon-decision-2022-faq |access-date=6 June 2022 |website=ATP Tour |publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref>
