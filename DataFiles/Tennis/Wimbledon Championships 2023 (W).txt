[[File:Wimbledon 2023 Semifinals (53044569246) (cropped).jpg|thumb|[[Markéta Vondroušová]] (pictured in semifinals) won the title.]]

[[Markéta Vondroušová]] defeated [[Ons Jabeur]] in the final, 6–4, 6–4 to win the ladies' singles tennis title at the [[2023 Wimbledon Championships]].<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/jul/15/vondrousova-the-disruptor-pulls-off-one-more-shock-to-win-wimbledon|title=  Vondroušová the disruptor pulls off one more shock to win Wimbledon|date=15 July 2023|work=Guardian|accessdate=17 July 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/66212165|title=  Marketa Vondrousova beats Ons Jabeur in women's final|date=15 July 2023|work=BBC Sport|accessdate=17 July 2023}}</ref>
It was her first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title, second career [[WTA Tour]] title and her first title overall since [[2017 Ladies Open Biel Bienne – Singles|2017]]. Vondroušová was the first [[Seed (sports)|unseeded]] woman to win the [[Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] title, the first to contest the final since [[Billie Jean King]] in [[1963 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|1963]],<ref>{{Cite news |last=Fendrich |first=Howard |date=13 July 2023 |title=Ons Jabeur reaches Wimbledon final in historic comeback victory |work=[[Los Angeles Times]] |agency=[[Associated Press]] |url=https://www.latimes.com/sports/story/2023-07-13/marketa-vondrousova-advances-wimbledon-final |url-status=live |access-date=13 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230713221631/https://www.latimes.com/sports/story/2023-07-13/marketa-vondrousova-advances-wimbledon-final |archive-date=13 July 2023}}</ref> and at world No. 42 was the lowest-ranked champion since the [[WTA rankings]] were established in 1975.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Nguyen |first=Courtney |date=13 July 2023 |title=Five things to know about Wimbledon finalist Marketa Vondrousova |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/3590132/five-things-to-know-about-wimbledon-finalist-marketa-vondrousova |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230713222325/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/3590132/five-things-to-know-about-wimbledon-finalist-marketa-vondrousova |archive-date=13 July 2023 |access-date=13 July 2023 |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]]}}</ref> By winning the title, Vondroušová made her debut in the top ten of the WTA rankings.

[[Elena Rybakina]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{Cite news |last=Futterman |first=Matthew |date=9 July 2022 |title=Elena Rybakina Wins Wimbledon and Her First Grand Slam Title |work=[[The New York Times]] |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2022/07/09/sports/tennis/rybakina-jabeur-wimbledon-russia.html |url-status=live |access-date=13 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220710005422/https://www.nytimes.com/2022/07/09/sports/tennis/rybakina-jabeur-wimbledon-russia.html |archive-date=10 July 2022}}</ref> but lost in the quarterfinals to Jabeur in a rematch of the previous year's final.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Parkinson |first=Hannah Jane |date=12 July 2023 |title=Ons Jabeur knocks out Elena Rybakina to avenge 2022 final defeat |work=[[The Guardian]] |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/jul/12/ons-jabeur-knocks-out-champion-elena-rybakina-revenge-2022-final-defeat-tennis-wimbledon |url-status=live |access-date=13 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230712172006/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/jul/12/ons-jabeur-knocks-out-champion-elena-rybakina-revenge-2022-final-defeat-tennis-wimbledon |archive-date=12 July 2023}}</ref> Her loss guaranteed a first-time Wimbledon champion for the sixth straight edition.<ref>{{Cite web |date=13 July 2023 |title=Wimbledon upset: Defending champion Elena Rybakina overpowered by Ons Jabeur in quarter-finals |url=https://olympics.com/en/news/wimbledon-upset-defending-champion-elena-rybakina-exit-ons-jabeur- |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230713224140/https://olympics.com/en/news/wimbledon-upset-defending-champion-elena-rybakina-exit-ons-jabeur- |archive-date=13 July 2023 |access-date=13 July 2023 |website=[[Olympic Games|Olympics]]}}</ref>

[[Iga Świątek]] and [[Aryna Sabalenka]] were in contention for the [[List of WTA number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]]. Świątek retained the No. 1 ranking after Sabalenka lost in the semifinals.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Carayol |first=Tumaini |date=13 July 2023 |title=Ons Jabeur digs deep to battle past Aryna Sabalenka into Wimbledon final |work=[[The Guardian]] |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/jul/13/ons-jabeur-digs-deep-to-battle-past-aryna-sabalenka-into-wimbledon-final |url-status=live |access-date=13 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230713183618/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/jul/13/ons-jabeur-digs-deep-to-battle-past-aryna-sabalenka-into-wimbledon-final |archive-date=13 July 2023}}</ref>

==Seeded players==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on WTA rankings as of 26 June 2023. Rankings and points before are as of 3 July 2023.

No ranking points were awarded for the 2022 tournament due to the [[Wimbledon ban on Russian and Belarusian players|ban on Russian and Belarusian players]].<ref>[https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/mar/31/wimbledon-to-allow-russian-and-belarusian-players-to-return-to-tournament-tennis Wimbledon allows Russian and Belarusian players back into tournament], ''The Guardian''</ref> However, because the tournament takes place one week later this year, players are defending points from tournaments that took place during the week of 11 July 2022 ([[2022 Ladies Open Lausanne – Singles|Lausanne]] and [[2022 Budapest Grand Prix – Singles|Budapest]]). Players who are not defending any points from those tournaments will have their 16th best result (shown in brackets in the table below) replaced with their points from the 2023 Wimbledon Championships.
