[[File:Aryna Sabalenka Trophy US Open 2024.jpg | thumb | right | Sabalenka lifting the 2024 US Open singles trophy ]]

[[Aryna Sabalenka]] defeated [[Jessica Pegula]] in the final, 7–5, 7–5 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2024 US Open (tennis)|2024 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4108753/sabalenka-overcomes-pegula-in-us-open-final-wins-third-grand-slam-title|title=Sabalenka overcomes Pegula in US Open final, wins third Grand Slam title|date=8 September 2024|website=WTA Tennis}}</ref> It was her first [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] singles title and third [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] singles title overall. Sabalenka was the fifth woman to win both [[hardcourt]] majors in the same year (having also won the [[2024 Australian Open – Women's singles|Australian Open]]) since the feat became achievable in 1988, after [[Steffi Graf]], [[Monica Seles]], [[Martina Hingis]] and [[Angelique Kerber]]. The final was a rematch of the [[2024 Cincinnati Open – Women's singles|Cincinnati]] final three weeks earlier, in which Sabalenka also prevailed.

[[Coco Gauff]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-09-10 |title=Coco Gauff battles back to stun Aryna Sabalenka and claim US Open title |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/sep/10/coco-gauff-battles-back-to-stun-aryna-sabalenka-and-claim-us-open-title |access-date=2024-08-20 |website=The Guardian }}</ref> but lost in the fourth round to [[Emma Navarro]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4105519/navarro-dashes-gauff-s-hopes-of-consecutive-us-open-crowns|title=Navarro dashes Gauff's hopes of consecutive US Open crowns|date=1 September 2024|website=WTA Tennis}}</ref>

This edition of the US Open had two of the three latest finishes in the history of the tournament. The third-round match between Sabalenka and [[Ekaterina Alexandrova]]
(which also set the record for the latest start in the history of the tournament beginning at 12:08 AM) was tied for the third-latest finish for a women's singles match at 1:48 AM.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4100999/sabalenka-comes-back-to-defeat-alexandrova-in-historic-late-night-us-open-win|title=Sabalenka comes back to defeat Alexandrova in historic late-night US Open win|date=31 August 2024|website=WTA Tennis}}</ref> The fourth round match between [[Zheng Qinwen]] and [[Donna Vekić]] set the new record for the latest finish of a U.S. Open's women's match at 2:15 AM.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.reuters.com/sports/tennis/zheng-defeats-vekic-us-opens-latest-finish-womens-match-2024-09-02/|title=Zheng defeats Vekic in US Open's latest finish for a women's match|date=2 September 2024|website=Reuters}}</ref>
