[[Ashleigh Barty]] defeated [[Markéta Vondroušová]] in the final, 6–1, 6–3 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2019 French Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2019/jun/08/ashleigh-barty-storms-to-french-open-glory-win-over-marketa-vondrousova|title =Ashleigh Barty storms to French Open glory with win over Vondrousova|date=8 June 2019|work=Guardian|access-date=11 June 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/48567958|title =French Open 2019: Ashleigh Barty beats Marketa Vondrousova to win title|date=8 June 2019|work=BBC Sport|access-date=11 June 2019}}</ref> It was her first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] singles title, making her the first Australian to win a [[French Open]] singles title since [[Margaret Court]] in [[1973 French Open – Women's singles|1973]] and the first to win any major singles title since [[Samantha Stosur]] at the [[2011 US Open – Women's singles|2011 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/barty-vondrousova-womens-singles-final-roland-garros|title=Ashleigh Barty: Roland-Garros champion|publisher=rolandgarros.com|date=8 June 2019|access-date=8 June 2019}}</ref>

[[Simona Halep]] was the defending champion, but lost to [[Amanda Anisimova]] in the quarterfinals. Anisimova was the first tennis player born in the 21st century to reach the quarterfinals and semifinals of a major.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/%E2%80%98i-cant-describe-how-excited-i-am%E2%80%99-anisimova-ends-bolsova%E2%80%99s-french-open-dream-gets-halep-maiden|title='I can't describe how excited I am': Anisimova ends Bolsova's French Open dream, gets Halep in maiden quarterfinal|publisher=WTA Tennis|first=Stephanie|last=Livaudais|date=3 June 2019|access-date=4 June 2019}}</ref>

[[Naomi Osaka]] retained the [[List of WTA number 1 ranked tennis players#Singles|world No. 1 singles ranking]] after [[Karolína Plíšková]] lost in the third round.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/no1-scenarios-pliskova-bertens-kvitovas-tough-road-unseat-osaka-roland-garros|title=No.1 Scenarios: Pliskova, Bertens, Kvitova's tough road to unseat Osaka at Roland Garros|author=Courtney Nguyen|publisher=WTA|date=23 May 2019|access-date=25 May 2019}}</ref> [[Angelique Kerber]], [[Kiki Bertens]] and [[Petra Kvitová]] were also in contention for the top ranking. Osaka's streak of 16 consecutive wins at the majors was broken when she lost to [[Kateřina Siniaková]] in the third round.

For the first time since the [[1978 Australian Open – Women's singles|1978 Australian Open]], none of the four semifinalists had previously reached a major singles final. Of the four, [[Johanna Konta]] was the only one who had previously reached a semifinal. Of Konta's seven appearances in the main draw of the French Open, this was the only edition where she progressed beyond the first round.

This marked the final major main draw appearance of former world No. 4 and [[2016 WTA Finals – Singles|2016 WTA Finals]] champion [[Dominika Cibulková]]; she was defeated by [[Aryna Sabalenka]] in the first round. It was the first French Open main draw appearance of future world No. 1 and four-time champion [[Iga Świątek]], who reached the fourth round and was defeated by Halep. It was also the first major appearance of future [[2022 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|Wimbledon]] champion [[Elena Rybakina]], who lost to Siniaková in the first round.
