{{further|2021 Wimbledon Championships}}

Two-time defending champion [[Novak Djokovic]] defeated [[Matteo Berrettini]] in the 3 hour and 24 minutes final, 6–7<sup>(4–7)</sup>, 6–4, 6–4, 6–3 to win the gentlemen's singles tennis title at the [[2021 Wimbledon Championships]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/57792226|title=Wimbledon 2021: Novak Djokovic beats Matteo Berrettini for 20th Grand Slam title|date=11 July 2021|publisher=BBC Sport|accessdate=19 October 2021}}</ref>
It was his sixth [[Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] title and record-equaling 20th [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] men's singles title overall, tying [[Roger Federer]] and [[Rafael Nadal]]'s all-time [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|record]] total.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2021/jul/11/novak-djokovic-wins-sixth-wimbledon-title-after-battle-with-matteo-berrettini|title=Novak Djokovic wins sixth Wimbledon title after battle with Matteo Berrettini|date=11 July 2021|work=Guardian|accessdate=13 July 2021}}</ref> Djokovic became the first man to win the [[Australian Open]], [[French Open]] and Wimbledon titles in the same calendar year since [[Rod Laver#Performance timeline|Rod Laver in 1969]], the second to achieve the [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Surface Slam|Surface Slam]] (winning majors on three different surfaces in a calendar year), after Nadal in [[2010 Rafael Nadal tennis season|2010]], and the fifth in the [[Open Era]] to win the [[Channel Slam]] (French Open—Wimbledon double).<ref>{{Cite web|last=Sharada|first=R.|title=From Rod Laver to Roger Federer: Here are the men who have won the 'Channel Slam'|url=https://scroll.in/field/842100/from-rod-laver-to-roger-federer-here-are-the-men-who-have-won-the-channel-slam|access-date=2021-06-27|website=Scroll.in}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Bhagavatula|first=Manoj|date=2021-07-11|title=Stats: Djokovic ties Federer, Nadal's 20 Grand Slams with 6th Wimbledon title|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/31800623/novak-djokovic-roger-federer-rafael-nadal-20-grand-slam-6-wimbledon|url-status=live|access-date=2021-07-11|website=ESPN.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210711171917/https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/31800623/novak-djokovic-roger-federer-rafael-nadal-20-grand-slam-6-wimbledon |archive-date=2021-07-11 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=2021|title=Factbox-Tennis-Djokovic's career milestones after winning his 20th Grand Slam title|url=https://wtvbam.com/2021/07/11/factbox-tennis-djokovics-career-milestones-after-winning-his-20th-grand-slam-title/|access-date=2021-07-11|website=WTVB}}</ref>

Djokovic and [[Daniil Medvedev]] were in contention for the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]]; Djokovic retained the top ranking when Medvedev lost in the fourth round.

This event marked the final professional singles appearance of 20-time major champion, eight-time Wimbledon champion, and former world No. 1 Roger Federer; he lost in the quarterfinals to [[Hubert Hurkacz]]. Federer was the oldest man in the Open Era to reach the Wimbledon quarterfinals, and the oldest man to reach a major quarterfinal since [[Ken Rosewall]] at the [[1977 Australian Open (December) – Men's singles|December 1977 Australian Open]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Federer Sweeps Past Gasquet For Third-Round Berth {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/federer-gasquet-wimbledon-2021-thursday|access-date=2021-07-01|website=ATP Tour}}</ref> His fourth-round victory marked 105 match wins at Wimbledon. The 18 quarterfinals and 119 matches he contested at Wimbledon are both tournament records.<ref>{{Cite news|title=Federer sets up SW19 match with Norrie|publisher=BBC Sport|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/57674964|access-date=2021-07-02}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{cite tweet |author=SportsCenter |author-link=SportsCenter |user=SportsCenter |number=1412144214197014529 |date=July 5, 2021 |title=Roger Federer oldest man in the Open Era to reach the quarterfinals at Wimbledon. https://t.co/0RO5bT9saa |access-date=July 12, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210709191745/https://twitter.com/sportscenter/status/1412144214197014529 |archive-date=July 9, 2021 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Berrettini was the first Italian man to reach a major final since [[Adriano Panatta]] at the [[1976 French Open – Men's singles|1976 French Open]] and the first to do so at Wimbledon.<ref>{{Cite news|title=Wimbledon 2021: Novak Djokovic to face Matteo Berrettini in men's final|publisher=BBC Sport |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/57779798|url-status=live|access-date=2021-07-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210709152817/https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/57779798 |archive-date=2021-07-09 }}</ref> [[Zhang Zhizhen]] was the first Chinese man to qualify for the Wimbledon main draw in the Open Era.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Zhizhen Zhang of China makes history|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2021-06-24/zhang_first_chinese_man_in_open_era_to_play_main_draw.html|url-status=live|access-date=2021-06-27|website=www.wimbledon.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210625000750/https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2021-06-24/zhang_first_chinese_man_in_open_era_to_play_main_draw.html |archive-date=2021-06-25 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Zhang Zhizhen Is First Chinese Man To Qualify For Wimbledon In The Open Era {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis|url=http://www.atptour.com/en/news/zhang-zhizhen-wimbledon-2021-qualifying-thursday|access-date=2021-06-27|website=ATP Tour}}</ref> [[Márton Fucsovics]] was the first Hungarian to reach a men's singles major quarterfinal since [[Balázs Taróczy]] at the [[1981 French Open – Men's singles|1981 French Open]], and the first to do so at Wimbledon since [[József Asbóth]] in [[1948 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|1948]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=The Ironman Of Tennis: Why Fucsovics is Tennis' Most Underrated Player|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fucsovics-roland-garros-2020-feature|url-status=live|access-date=2021-07-06|website=www.atptour.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201005141517/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fucsovics-roland-garros-2020-feature |archive-date=2020-10-05 }}</ref> This was the first time since [[2017 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2017]] that two-time champion [[Andy Murray]] participated.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Andy Murray receives wildcard to end 4-year Wimbledon singles absence|url=https://tennishead.net/andy-murray-receives-wildcard-to-end-4-year-wimbledon-singles-absence/|date=2021-06-21|access-date=2021-06-18|website=tennishead}}</ref>

For the first time since 2002, the [[The Championships, Wimbledon#Players and seeding|grass court seeding formula]] was abandoned and the standard ranking system based on the [[ATP rankings]] was permanently adopted, like at the other three majors.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/53365547|title=Wimbledon to give out £10m prize money for 2020 Championships|publisher=BBC Sport|date=10 July 2020|access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Jonathan|title=Wimbledon Drops Grass Court Seeding Formula For 2021 – peRFect Tennis|url=https://www.perfect-tennis.com/wimbledon-drops-grass-court-seeding-formula-for-2021/|access-date=25 June 2021|website=perfect-tennis.com}}</ref>

This marked the Wimbledon debut of future five-time major champion [[Carlos Alcaraz]] and four-time major champion [[Jannik Sinner]]. They lost in the second round and first round respectively, to Medvedev and Fucsovics.
