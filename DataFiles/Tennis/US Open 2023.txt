[[Novak Djokovic]] defeated [[Daniil Medvedev]] in the final, 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–5)</sup>, 6–3 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2023 US Open (tennis)|2023 US Open]]. It was his fourth [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] title and record-extending 24th men's singles [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall. Djokovic was the oldest US Open men's singles champion in the [[Open Era]], at 36 years and 111 days.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fendrich |first=Howard |date=2023-09-10 |title=Novak Djokovic wins the US Open for his 24th Grand Slam title by beating Daniil Medvedev |url=https://apnews.com/article/us-open-final-live-updates-djokovic-medvedev-8a4a26f8d77ef9ab2fb3efe1096dce7e |access-date=2023-09-10 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref> By reaching a 47th men's singles major semifinal, he surpassed [[Roger Federer]]'s all-time [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|record]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-fritz-us-open-2023-tuesday|title=Djokovic Extends Domination Against Americans, Reaches US Open SFs|date=5 September 2023|publisher=[[Association of Tennis Professionals]]}}</ref> and by reaching the final, he equaled Federer's record of reaching all four major finals in a season three times. By winning his first-round match, Djokovic regained the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]] at the end of the tournament; [[Carlos Alcaraz]] was also in contention for the top position.

Alcaraz was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-09-11/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_casper_ruud_for_2022_us_open_title_world_no_1_ranking.html|title=Carlos Alcaraz defeats Casper Ruud for 2022 US Open title, world No. 1 ranking|first=Arthur|last=Kapetanakis|date=11 September 2022|website=US Open|access-date=August 18, 2023|archive-date=September 12, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220912091653/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-09-11/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_casper_ruud_for_2022_us_open_title_world_no_1_ranking.html|url-status=live}}</ref> but he lost in the semifinals to Medvedev.

This tournament marked the last US Open appearance of [[2012 US Open – Men's singles|2012]] champion, three-time major champion, two-time Olympic gold medalist, and former world No. 1 [[Andy Murray]]; he lost in the second round to [[Grigor Dimitrov]]. It was also the final professional appearance of former world No. 8 and [[Ace (tennis)#Professional singles records|career-ace record-holder]] [[John Isner]];<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2023-08-23/john_isner_announces_plans_to_retire_following_2023_us_open.html | title=John Isner announces plans to retire following 2023 US Open }}</ref> he lost in the second round to [[Michael Mmoh]].
