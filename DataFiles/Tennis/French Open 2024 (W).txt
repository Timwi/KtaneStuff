Two-time defending champion [[Iga Świątek]]<ref>{{cite news |title=After Facing Her Biggest Challenge in a Final Yet, Iga Swiatek Wins Her Third French Open |url=https://www.vogue.com/article/iga-swiatek-wins-third-french-open-2023 |access-date=26 April 2024 |work=Vogue |date=10 June 2023}}</ref> defeated [[Jasmine Paolini]] in the final, 6–2, 6–1 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2024 French Open]]. It was her fourth [[French Open]] and fifth [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall. Świątek [[List of Grand Slam and related tennis records#Won a title after saving match points|saved a match point en route to the title]], in the second round against [[Naomi Osaka]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=29 May 2024 |title=Swiatek saves match point, overcomes Osaka in Roland Garros thriller |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4030259/swiatek-saves-match-point-overcomes-osaka-in-roland-garros-thriller |url-status=live |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20240608142824/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4030259/swiatek-saves-match-point-overcomes-osaka-in-roland-garros-thriller |archive-date=8 June 2024 |access-date=2024-06-08 |website=Women's Tennis Association |language=en}}</ref> She was the third woman in the [[Open Era]] to win three consecutive French Open titles (after [[Monica Seles]] and [[Justine Henin]]).<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4035542/champions-corner-the-swiatek-mantra-serve-win-trophy-repeat|title=Champions Corner: The Swiatek mantra – serve, win, trophy, repeat |date=9 June 2024|accessdate=10 June 2024 |website=WTAtennis.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Upasani |first1=Atharva |title=French Open 2024: Is Iga Swiatek the presumptive favourite for another Roland Garros title? |url=https://www.sportskeeda.com/tennis/french-open-2024-is-iga-swiatek-presumptive-favourite-another-roland-garros-title |access-date=16 May 2024 |work=Sportskeeda}}</ref> Świątek joined Seles and [[Margaret Court]] as only the third woman in the Open Era to win all of their first five major singles finals.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Jefford |first=Oli |date=14 July 2024 |title=The 5 players to win their first four Grand Slam finals: Carlos Alcaraz matches Roger Federer |url=https://www.tennis365.com/facts-stats/players-win-first-four-grand-slam-finals-carlos-alcaraz |url-status= |access-date=23 October 2024 |website=Tennis365}}</ref> The victory completed a calendar sweep for Świątek of the [[2024 Mutua Madrid Open – Women's singles|Madrid Open]], [[2024 Italian Open – Women's singles|Italian Open]], and French Open clay tournaments, becoming the first player to win all three major clay tournaments in a year since [[Serena Williams]] in [[2013 WTA Tour|2013]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Jacobs |first=Shahida |date=8 June 2024 |title=Incredible Iga Swiatek stats as Pole equals Serena Williams' rare feat with title run at Roland Garros |url=https://www.tennis365.com/french-open/incredible-iga-swiatek-stats-pole-equals-serena-williams-rare-feat-title-run-roland-garros |work=Tennis365 |no-pp=y}}</ref>

[[Mirra Andreeva]] was the youngest player to reach a major semifinal since [[Martina Hingis]] at the [[1997 US Open – Women's singles|1997 US Open]].<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-06-06/french-open-womens-mirra-andreeva-causes-huge-upset/103943002 | title=Teenager Mirra Andreeva causes major upset at French Open | newspaper=ABC news | accessdate=15 June 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Gray |first1=James |date=5 June 2024 |title=Mirra Andreeva, 17, shocks Aryna Sabalenka to reach first Grand Slam semi-final |url=https://inews.co.uk/sport/tennis/mirra-andreeva-shocks-aryna-sabalenka-first-grand-slam-semi-final-3094245 |accessdate=5 June 2024 |work=[[I (newspaper)|The i]]}}</ref> [[Alizé Cornet]] made her 69th and last consecutive major main draw appearance, the longest consecutive streak of major appearances by any woman.<ref>{{cite web |title=Au revoir, Alize Cornet |url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/au-revoir-alize-cornet |website=Roland-Garros |access-date=29 May 2024 |language=en-us}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=French Open 2024: 'Emotional' Alize Cornet ends career after record 69th straight Grand Slam |url=https://www.firstpost.com/sports/tennis-news/french-open-alize-cornet-retire-13776184.html |access-date=29 May 2024 |work=Firstpost |date=28 May 2024 |language=en-us}}</ref>
