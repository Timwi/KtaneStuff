{{further|2020 French Open}}

Three-time defending champion [[Rafael Nadal]] defeated [[Novak Djokovic]] in [[2020 French Open – Men's singles final|the final]], 6–0, 6–2, 7–5 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2020 French Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2019/jun/09/rafael-nadal-beats-dominic-thiem-wins-french-open-tennis-title|title=Rafael Nadal beats Dominic Thiem to win 12th French Open title|date=9 June 2019|work=Guardian|access-date=11 June 2019}}</ref> It was his record-extending 13th [[French Open]] title and 20th [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall, equaling [[Roger Federer]]'s all-time [[List of Grand Slam-related tennis records#Singles all-time totals|record]] of men's singles titles. For the fourth time in his career, Nadal [[List of Grand Slam related tennis records#Won a title without losing a set|did not lose a set during the tournament]] (following [[2008 French Open – Men's singles|2008]], [[2010 French Open – Men's singles|2010]], and [[2017 French Open – Men's singles|2017]]). For the first time in French Open history, neither the men's nor [[2020 French Open – Women's singles|women's]] singles champions lost a set during their tournaments.<ref name=Tenniscom>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com/pro-game/2020/10/rafa-completes-record-breaking-run-roland-garros/91369/|title=Nadal's Numbers: 100-2 at Roland Garros, 20 Slams, 13 Coupes ''[sic]'' - and More|date=11 October 2020|publisher=[[Tennis.com]]}}</ref> Nadal also became the first player, male or female, to win 100 matches at the French Open, and only the second man, after Federer at the [[Australian Open]] and [[The Championships, Wimbledon|Wimbledon]], to win 100 matches at the same major.<ref name=Tenniscom/>

Djokovic was attempting to become the first man in the [[Open Era]] to achieve a [[List of Grand Slam men's singles champions#Career Grand Slam|double career Grand Slam]], a feat he would accomplish the [[2021 French Open – Men's singles|following year]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2020/oct/11/rafael-nadal-demolishes-novak-djokovic-to-win-13th-french-open-title|title=Rafael Nadal demolishes Novak Djokovic to win 13th French Open title|date=11 October 2020|work=Guardian|access-date=12 October 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/54499164|title=French Open: Rafael Nadal beats Novak Djokovic to win 13th Roland Garros title|date=11 October 2020|work=BBC Sport|access-date=12 October 2020}}</ref> Instead, he suffered his worst-ever defeat in a major final, winning only seven games.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.cnn.com/2020/10/11/tennis/djokovic-nadal-french-open-tennis-spt-intl/index.html |title=Rafael Nadal beats Novak Djokovic at French Open for record tying 20th major |website=[[CNN]] |date=11 October 2020 }}</ref>

[[Taylor Fritz]] and [[Lorenzo Sonego]] played the longest tiebreak in French Open history in the third round: Sonego won the third set tiebreak 19–17.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/live/2020/oct/02/french-open-2020-dominic-thiem-simona-halep-rafael-nadal-day-six-live-tennis|title=Nadal and Wawrinka in action after Halep and Thiem win|first=Tumaini|last=Carayol|website=[[TheGuardian.com]] |date=2 October 2020}}</ref>

[[Jannik Sinner]] was the first man to reach the quarterfinals on his tournament debut since Nadal in [[2005 French Open – Men's singles|2005]], and the youngest quarterfinalist since Djokovic in [[2006 French Open – Men's singles|2006]]. He was the first man born in the 21st century to reach a major quarterfinal.<ref>{{cite news| title= Sinner reaches last 8 in 2020 French Open|newspaper=
Reuters|url=
https://www.reuters.com/article/us-tennis-frenchopen-sinner-idCAKBN26P0LQ|date=4 October 2020}}</ref> 

Nadal and Sinner's quarterfinal match was the first men's match to start at night in French Open history. Their match, played under cold and windy conditions, started after 10:00 pm CEST and finished at 1:26 am, the first French Open match to finish after midnight.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-03-18 |title=French Open 2020 - Rafael Nadal battles past plucky Jannik Sinner in near 1.30am finish |url=https://www.eurosport.com/tennis/roland-garros/2020/french-open-2020-rafael-nadal-battles-past-plucky-jannik-sinner-in-near-1.30am-finish_sto7934927/story.shtml |website=www.eurosport.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-10-06 |title=Rafael Nadal beats Jannik Sinner in latest French Open finish ever |url=https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/rafael-nadal-jannik-sinner-french-open |access-date=2025-07-24 |website=NBC Sports |language=en-US}}</ref>
