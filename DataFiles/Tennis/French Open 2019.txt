Two-time defending champion [[Rafael Nadal]] defeated [[Dominic Thiem]] in a rematch of the previous year's final, 6–3, 5–7, 6–1, 6–1 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2019 French Open]]. It was his record-extending twelfth [[French Open]] title and 18th [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/48574263 |title=Rafael Nadal beats Dominic Thiem to win 12th French Open title |date=9 June 2019 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=11 June 2019}}</ref> With the win, Nadal surpassed [[Margaret Court]]'s all-time record for the most singles titles by a player at the same major.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2019/jun/09/rafael-nadal-beats-dominic-thiem-wins-french-open-tennis-title|title =Rafael Nadal beats Dominic Thiem to win 12th French Open title|date=9 June 2019|work=Guardian|access-date=11 June 2019}}</ref>	

[[Novak Djokovic]] and [[Roger Federer]] were both attempting to complete [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Career Grand Slam|double career Grand Slam]], with Djokovic also in contention to achieve a [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Non-calendar-year Grand Slam|non-calendar-year Grand Slam]], but both lost in the semifinals. This was Federer's first time playing the French Open in four years. Federer's third round match marked his 400th major match, an all-time record.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/26863647/federer-wins-400th-career-grand-slam-match|title=Federer wins 400th career Grand Slam match|publisher=ESPN|date=31 May 2019 }}</ref> Federer was the oldest man to reach the fourth round at the French Open since [[Nicola Pietrangeli]] in [[1972 French Open – Men's singles|1972]],<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/us-tennis-frenchopen-federer/federer-beats-ruud-to-become-oldest-man-to-reach-french-open-fourth-round-idUSKCN1T11XJ|title='Golden oldie' Federer cruises into French Open 4th round|agency=Reuters|date=31 May 2019 }}</ref> and the oldest semifinalist since the 40-year-old [[Pancho Gonzales]] in [[1968 French Open – Men's singles|1968]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/federer-wawrinka-roland-garros-2019-tuesday|title=Federer Battles Past Wawrinka, Sets Nadal SF Clash At Roland Garros|publisher=ATP|date=4 June 2019 }}</ref>

The first round match between [[Ivo Karlović]] (40 years and three months) and [[Feliciano López]] (37 years and 8 months) was the oldest French Open men's singles match in terms of combined ages in the Open Era. Karlović was the oldest man to compete in the singles tournament since [[István Gulyás]] in [[1973 French Open – Men's singles|1973]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.reuters.com/article/us-tennis-frenchopen-karlovic/karlovic-40-beats-lopez-in-oldest-match-idUSKCN1SY1NE|title=Karlovic, 40, beats Lopez in oldest match |agency=Reuters |date=28 May 2019 }}</ref> [[Stefanos Tsitsipas]] was the first Greek to reach the round of 16 since Lazaros Stalios in [[1936 French Championships|1936]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.france24.com/en/20190601-tsitsipas-first-greek-83-years-reach-roland-garros-last-16|title=Tsitsipas first Greek in 83 years to reach Roland Garros last 16|date=June 1, 2019}}</ref>

For only the third time in the Open Era and the first time since the [[1970 Australian Open – Men's singles|1970 Australian Open]], all of the top 10 seeds reached the round of 16 at a men's singles major.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-nadal-federer-top-10-seeds-r16-roland-garros-2019|title=Djokovic, Nadal & Federer Lead Top 10 Seeds To Perfect 30 At Roland Garros |publisher=ATP Tour |date=1 June 2019 }}</ref> It was the first time since the [[2013 Australian Open – Men's singles|2013 Australian Open]] that the top four seeds (Djokovic, Nadal, Federer, and Thiem) all reached the semifinals of a major, and the first time since the [[2012 French Open – Men's singles|2012 French Open]] that the [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]] all reached the semifinals of a major.

This marked the final major appearance of [[2009 US Open – Men's singles|2009 US Open]] champion [[Juan Martín del Potro]], who lost to [[Karen Khachanov]] in the fourth round. Del Potro would retire from tennis in 2022 due to recurring injuries.
