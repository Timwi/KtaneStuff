{{main|2021 US Open (tennis)}}
[[Daniil Medvedev]] defeated [[Novak Djokovic]] in the final, 6–4, 6–4, 6–4 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2021 US Open (tennis)|2021 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2021/sep/12/daniil-medvedev-novak-djokovic-calendar-slam-dream-ended-us-open-mens-singles-final-tennis|title=Daniil Medvedev ends Novak Djokovic's Grand Slam dreams|website=[[TheGuardian.com]] |date=12 September 2021 |access-date=12 September 2021}}</ref> It was his first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title. Medvedev was the third Russian man, after [[Yevgeny Kafelnikov]] and [[Marat Safin]], to win a major singles title, and the first to do so since Safin at the [[2005 Australian Open – Men's singles|2005 Australian Open]].

Djokovic was aiming to become the second man in the [[Open Era]], after [[Rod Laver]] in 1969, to complete the [[Grand Slam (tennis)|Grand Slam]].<ref name="Calendar Slam">{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/us-open-resource-page-2021|title=Djokovic Targets Grand Slam At The US Open; All You Need To Know|work=[[ATP Tour]]|date=23 August 2021|access-date=24 August 2021|archive-date=25 August 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210825025052/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/us-open-resource-page-2021|url-status=live}}</ref> He was also attempting to win an outright record 21st major singles title and surpass his [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]] counterparts, [[Roger Federer]] and [[Rafael Nadal]].<ref name="Calendar Slam"></ref> By reaching the final, Djokovic tied Federer's [[List of Grand Slam–related tennis records|record]] of 31 men's singles major finals contested, and repeated his [[2015 Novak Djokovic tennis season|2015]] achievement of reaching all four major finals in a season. This marked Djokovic's sixth runners-up finish at the event, the most in one of men's grand slam events (tied with [[Bill Johnston (tennis player)|Bill Johnston]] who also had six at the US Open). This is also a record for the [[ATP Tour]] event.

[[Dominic Thiem]] was the reigning champion, but did not participate due to an ongoing wrist injury.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/32043941/defending-champion-dominic-thiem-miss-us-open-wrist-injury|title=Defending champion Dominic Thiem to miss US Open tennis tournament with wrist injury|website=[[ESPN]]|date=18 August 2021|access-date=19 August 2021|archive-date=19 August 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210819200546/https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/32043941/defending-champion-dominic-thiem-miss-us-open-wrist-injury|url-status=live}}</ref> This wrist injury would eventually lead to his retirement from the sport in 2024.

By defeating world No. 3 [[Stefanos Tsitsipas]] in the third round, [[Carlos Alcaraz]] became the youngest man to beat a top 3 ranked-player in singles at the [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] since the inception of the [[ATP rankings]] in 1973. He was the youngest male quarterfinalist at a singles major since [[Michael Chang]] at the [[1989 French Open – Men's singles|1989 French Open]], and the youngest at the US Open in the Open Era; he would win the title [[2022 US Open – Men's singles|the following year]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/58452074|title=US Open: Spanish teenager Carlos Alcaraz wins in five sets to reach quarter-finals|work=[[BBC Sport]]|date=5 September 2021|access-date=5 September 2021|archive-date=5 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210905213716/https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/58452074|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Botic van de Zandschulp]] was the first Dutchman to reach a singles major quarterfinal since [[Sjeng Schalken]] at the [[2004 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2004 Wimbledon Championships]] and the first qualifier to do so at the US Open since [[Gilles Müller]] in [[2008 US Open – Men's singles|2008]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.atptour.com/en/news/felix-alcaraz-medvedev-us-open-2021-day-9-preview-tuesday|title=Felix Auger-Aliassime & Carlos Alcaraz Meet; Daniil Medvedev, Botic Van De Zandschulp Eye Semi-final Spots {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis|work=[[ATP Tour]]|access-date=7 September 2021|archive-date=7 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210907062403/https://www.atptour.com/en/news/felix-alcaraz-medvedev-us-open-2021-day-9-preview-tuesday|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Fernandez|first=Megan|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2021-09-07/by_the_numbers_botic_van_de_zandschulps_big_breakthrough_at_the_2021_us_open.html|title=By the Numbers: Botic van de Zandschulp's big breakthrough at the 2021 US Open|website=usopen.org|date=9 September 2021|access-date=7 September 2021|archive-date=7 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210907115204/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2021-09-07/by_the_numbers_botic_van_de_zandschulps_big_breakthrough_at_the_2021_us_open.html|url-status=live}}</ref>
