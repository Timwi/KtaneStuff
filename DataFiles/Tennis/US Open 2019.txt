{{main article|2019 US Open (tennis)}}

[[Rafael Nadal]] defeated [[Daniil Medvedev]] in the final, 7–5, 6–3, 5–7, 4–6, 6–4 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2019 US Open (tennis)|2019 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/49629579|title=US Open 2019: Rafael Nadal beats Daniil Medvedev to win 19th Grand Slam title|publisher=BBC Sport|accessdate=16 July 2025}}</ref> It was his fourth [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] title and 19th [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournament|major]] title overall. Nadal's victory meant that every major title since the beginning of [[2017 ATP World Tour|2017]] had been won by either himself (5), [[Novak Djokovic]] (4) or [[Roger Federer]] (3). This was the first time since 2006–08 that [[Big Three (tennis)|Djokovic, Federer, and Nadal]] had claimed all four major singles titles across three consecutive years.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/live/2019/sep/08/us-open-mens-final-rafael-nadal-v-daniil-medvedev-live |title =Rafael Nadal beats Daniil Medvedev to win US Open men's final – as it happened|date=8 September 2019|work=Guardian|access-date=10 September 2019}}</ref>

Djokovic was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2019-07-17/2019-07-16_djokovic_nadal_federer_headline_2019_us_open_mens_singles_field.html|title=US Open Men's Singles Overview|date=17 July 2019}}</ref> but retired due to a left shoulder injury against [[Stan Wawrinka]] in the fourth round. This was the first time since [[2006 US Open – Men's singles|2006]] that Djokovic failed to reach the US Open semifinals (not counting the [[2017 US Open – Men's singles|2017 tournament]], which he missed due to injury).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-wawrinka-us-open-2019-sunday|title=Wawrinka Moves Past Djokovic At The US Open|publisher=ATP Tour|date=1 September 2019}}</ref>

Medvedev was the second Russian player (after [[Marat Safin]]) to reach the final, and the first Russian man to reach a major final since Safin at the [[2005 Australian Open – Men's singles|2005 Australian Open]].{{Citation needed|date=July 2025}}

[[Grigor Dimitrov]] (ranked 78th) was the lowest-ranked US Open men's semifinalist since [[Jimmy Connors]] (ranked 174th) in [[1991 US Open – Men's singles|1991]], and the lowest-ranked men's singles semifinalist at any major since [[Rainer Schüttler]] (ranked 92nd) at the [[2008 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2008 Wimbledon Championships]]. This was the first time in the Open Era that four quarterfinalists (Federer, Nadal, Wawrinka and [[Gaël Monfils]]) were 33 years old or older.{{cn|date=September 2019}}

This marked the first major main-draw appearance for future major champion and world No. 1 [[Jannik Sinner]]; he lost to Wawrinka in the first round.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/wawrinka-muscles-past-talented-18-year-old-qualifier-jannik-sinner|title=Wawrinka muscles past talented 18-year-old qualifier Jannik Sinner|publisher=tennis.com|accessdate=16 July 2025}}</ref> This was the final US Open appearance of five-time champion Federer; he lost in the quarterfinals to Dimitrov.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2019/sep/04/us-open-roger-federer-grigor-dimitrov|title=Ailing Roger Federer's US Open run ended as Grigor Dimitrov springs upset|publisher=|accessdate=16 July 2025}}</ref>
