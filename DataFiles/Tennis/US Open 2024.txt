[[Jannik Sinner]] defeated [[Taylor Fritz]] in the final, 6–3, 6–4, 7–5 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2024 US Open (tennis)|2024 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-fritz-us-open-2024-final|title=Sinner surges on! World No. 1 defeats home hope Fritz for US Open title |date=9 September 2024|website=ATP Tour}}</ref> It was his second [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title. Sinner became the fourth (and youngest) man to win both [[hardcourt]] singles majors ([[Australian Open]] and [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]]) in the same year, after [[Mats Wilander]], [[Roger Federer]] and [[Novak Djokovic]], and the first to win his first two major singles titles in the same year since [[Guillermo Vilas]] in 1977.

Djokovic was the defending champion, but lost in the third round to [[Alexei Popyrin]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-09-10 |title=Novak Djokovic wins the US Open for his 24th Grand Slam title by beating Daniil Medvedev |url=https://apnews.com/article/us-open-final-live-updates-djokovic-medvedev-8a4a26f8d77ef9ab2fb3efe1096dce7e |access-date=2024-08-20 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref> This marked Djokovic's earliest defeat at the US Open since [[2006 US Open – Men's singles|2006]], and earliest defeat at any major since the [[2017 Australian Open – Men's singles|2017 Australian Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/41062719/novak-djokovic-upset-alexei-popyrin-3rd-round-us-open|title=Novak Djokovic upset by Alexei Popyrin in 3rd round of US Open|date=30 August 2024|website=ESPN}}</ref> It was the first year since [[2002 ATP Tour|2002]] that none of the [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]] won a major, and the first major since the [[2004 French Open – Men's singles|2004 French Open]] without any of the Big Three in the round of 16.<ref>{{cite tweet|url=https://x.com/espn/status/1829728599840059607|number=1829728599840059607|date=30 August 2024|user=ESPN|title=ALEXEI POPYRIN COMPLETES THE UPSET OVER NOVAK DJOKOVIC 😱 This is the first major since the 2004 French Open without Djokovic, Nadal or Federer in the Round of 16 😳}}</ref>

The semifinal between Fritz and [[Frances Tiafoe]] marked the first all-American major men's semifinal since [[Andre Agassi]] defeated [[Robby Ginepri]] at the [[2005 US Open – Men's singles|2005 US Open]], with Fritz becoming the first American man to reach a major final since [[Andy Roddick]] at the [[2009 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2009 Wimbledon Championships]], and the first to do so at the US Open since Roddick in 2006.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fritz-tiafoe-us-open-2024-american-history|title=Fritz & Tiafoe make American history at US Open|date=3 September 2024|website=ATPTour}}</ref>

This tournament marked the final major appearance of [[2020 US Open – Men's singles|2020]] champion and former world No. 3 [[Dominic Thiem]]. He lost in the first round to [[Ben Shelton]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/report-dominic-thiem-retire-vienna-october-novak-djokovic-applauds-austrian-good-person|title= REPORT: Dominic Thiem to retire at Vienna in October; Novak Djokovic applauds Austrian}}</ref>

The first-round match between [[Dan Evans (tennis)|Dan Evans]] and [[Karen Khachanov]] was the longest match in the tournament's history at 5 hours and 35 minutes, surpassing the previous record of 5 hours and 26 minutes from the [[1992 US Open – Men's singles|1992]] semifinal between [[Stefan Edberg]] and [[Michael Chang]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/khachanov-evans-us-open-2024-longest-match|title=Evans beats Khachanov in longest match in US Open history; ''Match lasts five hours, 35 minutes''|date=27 August 2024|website=ATPTour}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/best-of-2024-grand-slam-comebacks|title= Longest US Open match in history headlines best Slam comebacks of 2024|date=5 December 2024|website=ATPTour}} </ref>
