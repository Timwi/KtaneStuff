[[Iga Świątek]] defeated [[Ons Jabeur]] in the final, 6–2, 7–6<sup>(7–5)</sup> to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2022 US Open (tennis)|2022 US Open]]. It was her first [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] title and third [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall. Świątek was the first player since [[Angelique Kerber]] in [[2016 WTA Tour|2016]] to win multiple majors in a season, having also won the [[2022 French Open – Women's singles|French Open]]. She became the first Polish woman in the [[Open Era]] to reach the US Open quarterfinals and beyond.{{efn-lr|[[Jadwiga Jędrzejowska]] reached the final in [[1937 U.S. National Championships – Women's singles|1937]], before the Open Era.}} Świątek was the youngest woman to win three majors since [[Maria Sharapova]] in 2008.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Swiatek outlasts Jabeur to win US Open; third career Slam title |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2787921/swiatek-outlasts-jabeur-to-win-us-open-third-career-slam-title |access-date=2023-09-11 |website=Women's Tennis Association |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Berkok |first=John |date=2022-09-16 |title=10 incredible things Iga Swiatek achieved by winning the US Open |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/10-incredible-things-iga-swiatek-achieved-by-winning-us-open-stats |access-date=2023-09-11 |website=Tennis.com |language=en}}</ref>

[[Emma Raducanu]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ausopen.com/articles/news/teenager-raducanu-wins-fairytale-us-open-title|title=Teenager Raducanu wins fairytale US Open title|first=Matt|last=Trollope|date=12 September 2021|website=[[Australian Open]]|access-date=20 July 2022}}</ref> but lost in the first round to [[Alizé Cornet]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/us-open-tennis-championships-sports-angelique-kerber-iga-swiatek-emma-raducanu-d9142d511204bf0ac1c1821ec810dc67|title=Raducanu's US Open title defense ends with loss to Cornet|last=Fendrich|first=Howard|date=31 August 2022|website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=31 August 2022}}</ref> It was the third time the defending US Open champion lost in the first round, after [[Svetlana Kuznetsova]] in [[2005 US Open – Women's singles|2005]] and Kerber in [[2017 US Open – Women's singles|2017]]. This marked the second consecutive major where Cornet defeated a reigning major champion (following her victory over Świątek at [[2022 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|Wimbledon]]). Cornet made her 63rd consecutive major appearance, surpassing [[Ai Sugiyama]]'s [[List of Grand Slam related tennis records#Participation|all-time record]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Cornet ends Raducanu's US Open title defense in first round |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2770451/cornet-ends-raducanu-s-us-open-title-defense-in-first-round |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220904114153/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2770451/cornet-ends-raducanu-s-us-open-title-defense-in-first-round |archive-date=4 September 2022 |access-date= |website=wtatennis.com}}</ref>

This tournament marked the final professional appearance of 23-time singles major champion, Olympic singles gold medalist, and former world No. 1 [[Serena Williams]]; she lost to [[Ajla Tomljanović]] in the third round.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vogue.com/article/serena-williams-retirement-in-her-own-words|title=Serena Williams Says Farewell to Tennis On Her Own Terms—And In Her Own Words|work=[[Vogue (magazine)|Vogue]]|author=[[Serena Williams|Williams, Serena]]|date=August 9, 2022|access-date=August 9, 2022}}</ref> It was the longest match at a major in Williams' career, lasting three hours and five minutes.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://tenniscompanion.org/longest-match-ever/#:~:text=Interestingly%2C%20the%20last%20match%20of,three%20hours%20and%20five%20minutes.|title=10+ Longest Tennis Matches Ever|date=3 November 2022|access-date=March 1, 2023}}</ref> With her victory over second-ranked [[Anett Kontaveit]] in the second round, Williams, at 40 years old, became the oldest player to defeat a top three opponent on the [[WTA Tour]].

This tournament was also the final major appearance of two-time major champion and former world No. 1 [[Simona Halep]]; she lost to [[Daria Snigur]] in the first round. Following the tournament, Halep served a provisional suspension for violations of anti-doping rules,<ref>{{Cite web |date=19 May 2023 |title=Former world No. 1 Simona Halep hit with second doping charge amid suspension|url=https://sports.yahoo.com/former-world-no-1-simona-halep-hit-with-second-doping-charge-amid-suspension-230728633.html |website=sports.yahoo.com}}</ref> which ended in March 2024. She announced her retirement from professional tennis in 2025.

This tournament marked the first time in the Open Era that four Chinese women reached the third round of a major.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Zheng Qinwen leads Chinese quartet making history at the US Open |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2775338/zheng-qinwen-leads-chinese-quartet-making-history-at-the-us-open |access-date=2022-09-05 |website=Women's Tennis Association |language=en}}</ref> [[Elise Mertens]] had her streak of 18 consecutive major third round appearances broken after losing in the first round to [[Irina-Camelia Begu]].

This was the first edition of the US Open to feature a [[Tennis scoring system#Scoring a tiebreak game|10-point tiebreak]], when the score reached six games all in the deciding set.<ref>{{cite web |date=16 March 2022 |title=US Open to join all Grand Slams in playing 10-point final set tiebreak |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-03-16/us_open_to_join_all_grand_slams_in_playing_10point_final_set_tiebreak.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://archive.today/20220901221942/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2022-02-28/2022-02-28_2022-02-28_reigning_us_open_champion_daniil_medvedev_breaks_new_ground_as_world_no_1.html |archive-date=1 September 2022 |access-date= |website=US Open}}</ref> [[Karolína Plíšková]] defeated [[Magda Linette]] in the first round in the first women's singles main draw 10-point tiebreak at the US Open.
