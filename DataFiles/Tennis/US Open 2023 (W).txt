[[Coco Gauff]] defeated [[Aryna Sabalenka]] in the final, 2–6, 6–3, 6–2 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2023 US Open (tennis)|2023 US Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/sep/10/coco-gauff-battles-back-to-stun-aryna-sabalenka-and-claim-us-open-title|title=Coco Gauff battles back to stun Aryna Sabalenka and claim US Open title|date=10 September 2023|work=Guardian|accessdate=11 September 2023}}</ref> It was her first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] singles title. Gauff was the first American teenager to win the title since [[Serena Williams]] in [[1999 US Open – Women's singles|1999]], and one of three teenagers to win the title within five years (following [[Bianca Andreescu]] in [[2019 US Open – Women's singles|2019]] and [[Emma Raducanu]] in [[2021 US Open – Women's singles|2021]]).<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=2023-09-09 |title=Stat of the Day: Coco Gauff becomes youngest American woman to win US Open since Serena Williams |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/stat-of-the-day-coco-gauff-youngest-american-woman-to-win-us-open-since-serena |access-date=2023-09-09 |website=Tennis.com |language=en}}</ref> Sabalenka was the first woman since Williams in [[2016 WTA Tour|2016]] to reach at least the semifinals of all four majors in a season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Berkok |first=John |date=2023-09-11 |title=Ranking Reaction: Djokovic and Sabalenka ascend to No. 1, Coco rises to No. 3 |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/ranking-reaction-djokovic-and-sabalenka-ascend-to-no-1-coco-rises-to-no-3 |access-date=2023-09-11 |website=Tennis.com |language=en}}</ref>

[[Iga Świątek]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web |last=Carayol |first=Tumaini |date=September 10, 2022 |title=Iga Swiatek holds off Ons Jabeur rally to win US Open women’s singles |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2022/sep/10/iga-swiatek-wins-us-open-womens-singles-beating-ons-jabeur |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230811201546/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2022/sep/10/iga-swiatek-wins-us-open-womens-singles-beating-ons-jabeur |archive-date=August 11, 2023 |accessdate=September 5, 2023 |work=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref> but lost to [[Jeļena Ostapenko]] in the fourth round.<ref name="ranking">{{Cite web |last=Carayol |first=Tumaini |date=September 4, 2023 |title=Iga Swiatek’s US Open title defence ended by Jelena Ostapenko |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/sep/04/us-open-2023-results-iga-swiatek-vs-jelena-ostapenko |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230905100836/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/sep/04/us-open-2023-results-iga-swiatek-vs-jelena-ostapenko |archive-date=September 5, 2023 |access-date=September 5, 2023 |website=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref> Her defeat guaranteed a first-time [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] champion. As a result of Świątek's fourth-round defeat, Sabalenka claimed the [[List of WTA number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]] for the first time in her career.<ref name="ranking" />

The third-round match between [[Caroline Wozniacki]], ranked No. 623, and [[Jennifer Brady]], ranked No. 433 (both playing their first majors in over two years), was the first third-round match in any major between two players outside the top 300 in singles rankings, male or female.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Drucker |first=Joel |date=September 1, 2023 |title=Caroline Wozniacki bests Jennifer Brady in US Open battle of players on the comeback trail |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/caroline-wozniacki-bests-jennifer-brady-us-open-battle-players-comeback-trail |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230905214516/https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/caroline-wozniacki-bests-jennifer-brady-us-open-battle-players-comeback-trail |archive-date=September 5, 2023 |access-date=September 5, 2023 |website=[[Tennis (magazine)|Tennis]]}}</ref> This was the first major since the [[2019 US Open – Women's singles|2019 US Open]] in which all eight quarterfinalists were seeded.
