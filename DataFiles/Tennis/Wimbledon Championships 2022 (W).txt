{{main|2022 Wimbledon Championships}}

[[Elena Rybakina]] defeated [[Ons Jabeur]] in the final, 3–6, 6–2, 6–2 to win the ladies' singles tennis title at the [[2022 Wimbledon Championships]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Elena Rybakina storms back to beat Ons Jabeur in big-hitting Wimbledon final|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2022/jul/09/elena-rybakina-storms-back-to-beat-ons-jabeur-in-wimbledon-final|work=The Guardian|date=9 July 2022|access-date=12 July 2022}}</ref> It was her first [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title, third career [[WTA Tour]] title and first career title on [[grass court]]s.<ref>{{cite web|first=Reem|last=Abulleil|title=New champion Rybakina plays it cool|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2022-07-09/rybakina_is_ladies_singles_champion.html|website=Wimbledon|date=9 July 2022|access-date=9 July 2022}}</ref> Rybakina was the first Kazakhstani{{efn-lr|Rybakina previously represented Russia before switching her nationality to Kazakhstan in 2018.}} to win a major, and the third player representing an Asian nation to win a major after [[Li Na]] and [[Naomi Osaka]]. She was the first player to win from a set down in the final since [[Amélie Mauresmo]] in [[2006 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2006]]. Jabeur was the first Arab to reach a major singles final, the first African woman to do so in the [[Open Era]], and the first African woman to do so not representing South Africa.<ref>{{cite web |date=7 July 2022 |title=Ons Jabeur reaches first Grand Slam final at Wimbledon |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/7/7/ons-jabeur-reaches-first-grand-slam-final-at-wimbledon |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220708035156/https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/7/7/ons-jabeur-reaches-first-grand-slam-final-at-wimbledon |archive-date=8 July 2022 |access-date=8 July 2022 |publisher=[[Al Jazeera Media Network|Al Jazeera]]}}</ref>{{efn-lr|The only other African women to contest major singles finals were South African: [[Irene Bowder Peacock]] at the [[1927 French Championships – Women's singles|1927 French Championships]], [[Renée Schuurman]] at the [[1959 Australian Championships – Women's singles|1959 Australian Championships]] and [[Sandra Reynolds]] at the [[1960 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|1960 Wimbledon Championships]].}} This marked the first time since [[2009 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2009]] that both finalists represented non-European nations.

[[Ashleigh Barty]] was the reigning champion, but she retired from professional tennis in March 2022.<ref>{{cite web |last=Garber |first=Greg  |title=World No.1, three-time Grand Slam winner Ashleigh Barty announces retirement |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2546898/world-no-1-three-time-grand-slam-winner-ashleigh-barty-announces-retirement |url-status=live |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]] |date=23 March 2022 |access-date=23 March 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220323083048/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2546898/world-no-1-three-time-grand-slam-winner-ashleigh-barty-announces-retirement |archive-date=23 March 2022}}</ref>

World No. 1 [[Iga Świątek]]'s win streak of 37 matches (dating back to [[2022 Qatar Total Open – Singles|Qatar]] in February) ended when she was defeated by [[Alizé Cornet]] in the third round. It was just the fourth loss of [[2022 Iga Świątek tennis season|Świątek's season]]; her 37-match winning streak is the longest of the 21st century, and tied for the 12th-longest in the Open Era.<ref name=":0">{{cite web |date=2 July 2022 |title=Cornet ends Swiatek's 37-match win streak; reaches fourth round at Wimbledon |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2665498/cornet-ends-swiatek-s-37-match-win-streak-reaches-fourth-round-at-wimbledon |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220703120803/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2665498/cornet-ends-swiatek-s-37-match-win-streak-reaches-fourth-round-at-wimbledon |archive-date=3 July 2022 |access-date=7 July 2022 |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Berkok |first=John |date=4 June 2022 |title=Stat of the day: Iga Swiatek joins rare Open Era company with 35-match winning streak |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/stat-of-the-day-iga-swiatek-joins-rare-open-era-company-with-35-match-winning-st |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220708004522/https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/stat-of-the-day-iga-swiatek-joins-rare-open-era-company-with-35-match-winning-st |archive-date=8 July 2022 |access-date=8 July 2022 |website=[[Tennis (magazine)|Tennis]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=29 May 1983 |title=Navratilova Upset By Horvath, 17 |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/sports/1983/05/29/navratilova-upset-by-horvath-17/103c6211-face-41a0-ad15-0f463f2a5ecf/ |access-date=2 July 2022 |newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]}}</ref>

For the second time in her career, Cornet defeated the incumbent world No. 1 in the third round at [[Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]]; she had defeated [[Serena Williams]] at that stage in [[2014 Wimbledon Championships - Women's singles|2014]].<ref name=":0" /> By making her 62nd consecutive major main draw appearance, Cornet tied [[Ai Sugiyama]]'s [[List of Grand Slam related tennis records#Participation|all-time record]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Rothenberg |first=Ben |date=23 January 2022 |title=A Grand Slam Streak Without (Yet) a Quarterfinal |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2022/01/23/sports/tennis/alize-cornet-australian-open.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429224151/https://www.nytimes.com/2022/01/23/sports/tennis/alize-cornet-australian-open.html |archive-date=29 April 2022 |access-date=22 June 2022 |website=[[The New York Times]]}}</ref>

Williams received a wild card to play in the tournament, making her comeback to the sport after a year away due to a right hamstring injury;<ref>{{cite web |last1=Mngqosini |first1=Sammy |last2=Wyatt |first2=Amy |date=14 June 2022 |title=Serena Williams handed wild card entry for Wimbledon return |url=https://edition.cnn.com/2022/06/14/tennis/serena-williams-wimbledon-return-intl-spt/index.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220627194949/https://edition.cnn.com/2022/06/14/tennis/serena-williams-wimbledon-return-intl-spt/index.html |archive-date=27 June 2022 |access-date=14 June 2022 |publisher=[[CNN]]}}</ref> she was defeated in the first round by [[Harmony Tan]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Oxley |first=Sonia |date=28 June 2022 |title=Serena Williams loses to Harmony Tan on Wimbledon return after year out |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/61971291 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704123834/https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/61971291 |archive-date=4 July 2022 |access-date=3 July 2022 |publisher=[[BBC Sport]]}}</ref> This would be the seven-time champion's last appearance at the tournament, as she retired from professional tennis after the [[2022 US Open – Women's singles|2022 US Open]]. This was also the final Wimbledon appearance for [[2019 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2019]] champion and former world No. 1 [[Simona Halep]]; she lost to Rybakina in the semifinals.

This was the first edition of Wimbledon to feature a champions [[Glossary of tennis terms#T|tiebreak]], a 10-point tiebreak when the score reaches six games all in the third set,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2022-03-16/grand_slam_tournaments_jointly_announce_10point_final_set_tiebreak_at_six_games_all.html|title=Grand Slam Tournaments Jointly Announce 10-Point Final Set Tie-Break at Six Games All|date=16 March 2022|access-date=3 June 2022|website=The Championships, Wimbledon}}</ref> and the third edition to feature a final-set tiebreak rule.{{efn-lr|The previous tie-break rule was a 7-point tie-break, when the score reaches twelve games all in the third set.}} The first women's singles main draw match of the tournament to feature the 10-point tiebreak was the first round match between [[Caroline Garcia]] and [[Yuriko Miyazaki]], with Garcia emerging victorious.<ref>{{cite news |last=Dickson Jefford |first=Oli |date=27 June 2022 |title=Wimbledon 2022: Lily Miyazaki targets top 100 after 'amazing' Wimbledon debut |work=[[Yahoo! News]] |url=https://news.yahoo.com/wimbledon-2022-lily-miyazaki-targets-top-100-after-amazing-wimbledon-debut-200823644.html |url-status=live |access-date=7 July 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220627202334/https://news.yahoo.com/wimbledon-2022-lily-miyazaki-targets-top-100-after-amazing-wimbledon-debut-200823644.html |archive-date=27 June 2022}}</ref>

==Seeded players==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on WTA rankings as of 20 June 2022. Rankings and points are as before 27 June 2021.

The WTA decided not to award ranking points for the 2022 tournament in response to the [[All England Club]]'s decision to [[Wimbledon ban on Russian and Belarusian players|ban Russian and Belarusian players]] from participating. Points dropping from the 2021 tournament will accordingly be replaced by the player's next best result, regardless of her performance at Wimbledon in 2022. <ref>{{Cite web |last=Simon |first=Steve |date=20 May 2022 |title=Statement from Steve Simon on UK grass-court events |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2626269/statement-from-steve-simon-on-uk-grass-court-events |access-date= |website=WTA Tour |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]] |language=en}}</ref> Note that 2021 points were mandatory for WTA players, unlike for ATP players, who were subject to a different rankings adjustment system.
