{{further|2021 French Open}}

[[Novak Djokovic]] defeated [[Stefanos Tsitsipas]] in the final, 6–7<sup>(6–8)</sup>, 2–6, 6–3, 6–2, 6–4, to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2021 French Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2021/jun/13/french-open-final-report-novak-djokovic-stefanos-tsitsipas|title=Novak Djokovic rallies to beat Stefanos Tsitsipas in epic French Open final|date=13 June 2021|work=Guardian|accessdate=18 June 2021}}</ref> It was his second [[French Open]] title and 19th [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall. Djokovic became the first man to achieve the [[Open Era tennis records – Men's singles#Career Grand Slam|double career Grand Slam]] in the [[Open Era]],<ref>{{Cite news|title=Mission complete: Djokovic secures extremely rare feat|url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/mission-complete-djokovic-secures-extremely-rare-feat-rg2021|work=Roland-Garros|access-date=2021-06-22}}{{Dead link|date=February 2022 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> and the first player in the Open Era to win a major after coming back from two-sets-to-love down in two matches during the same major.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Petrequin|first=Samuel|date=13 June 2021|title=Comeback! Djokovic tops Tsitsipas at French Open for Slam 19|url=https://apnews.com/article/europe-tennis-french-open-djokovic-comeback-tsitsipas-740f48e06b09d811787785a6be19e033|url-status=live|access-date=13 June 2021|website=AP NEWS|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210613191123/https://apnews.com/article/europe-tennis-french-open-djokovic-comeback-tsitsipas-740f48e06b09d811787785a6be19e033 |archive-date=2021-06-13 }}</ref> Tsitsipas was the first Greek to reach a major final.<ref name="Presse 2021">{{cite web | last=Presse | first=AFP – Agence France | title=Tsitsipas Becomes First Greek To Reach Grand Slam Final At French Open | website=Barron's | date=2021-06-11 | url=https://www.barrons.com/news/tsitsipas-becomes-first-greek-to-reach-grand-slam-final-at-french-open-01623430814 | access-date=2021-06-13}}</ref> It also marked the second consecutive year a man trailed by two sets in a major final yet rallied to win, following [[Dominic Thiem]]'s victory at the [[2020 US Open – Men's singles|2020 US Open]]. It was the first time since [[2004 French Open – Men's singles|2004]] that the final went to five sets; on that occasion, [[Gastón Gaudio]] also came from two sets down to win the title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nbcsports.com/northwest/novak-djokovic-beats-stefanos-tsitsipas-2021-french-open|title=Novak Djokovic beats Stefanos Tsitsipas in 2021 French Open|publisher=NBC Sports|first=Kelley|last=Ebert|date=14 June 2021|accessdate=15 June 2021}}</ref>

[[Rafael Nadal]] was the four-time defending champion, but lost to Djokovic in the semifinals.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/twitter-reacts|title=Nadal and Djokovic Play Out Classic}}</ref> Nadal's defeat marked only his third loss out of 108 French Open matches, including his first loss in 14 semifinal appearances at the tournament.<ref name="AP NEWS 2021">{{cite web | title=Djokovic hands Nadal 3rd loss in 108 French Open matches | website=AP NEWS | date=2021-06-11 | url=https://apnews.com/article/europe-french-open-tennis-sports-b6769b3b99a8e5fa74b605382cb057b1 | access-date=2021-06-13}}</ref><ref name="Reuters 2021">{{cite web | title=Nadal concedes best player won after losing to Djokovic in Paris | website=Reuters | date=2021-06-11 | url=https://www.reuters.com/lifestyle/sports/nadal-concedes-best-player-won-after-losing-djokovic-paris-2021-06-11/ | access-date=2021-06-13}}</ref> Djokovic became the only man to defeat Nadal twice at the French Open, and the only man to win the title after defeating him. It was [[Djokovic–Nadal rivalry|the pair]]'s record-extending 58th meeting.<ref name="ATP Tour 2021">{{cite web | title=Novak Djokovic & Rafael Nadal: The Rivalry - Tennis | website=ATP Tour | date=2021-06-12 | url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/rivalries-nadal-djokovic | access-date=2021-06-14}}</ref>

Djokovic and [[Daniil Medvedev]] were in contention for the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 ranking]]; Djokovic retained the top position after Medvedev lost in the quarterfinals.<ref name="Tennis.com 2021">{{cite web | title=Stat of the Day: Novak Djokovic clinches stay at No. 1 | website=Tennis.com | date=2021-06-08 | url=https://www.tennis.com/baseline/articles/stat-of-the-day-novak-djokovic-clinches-stay-at-no-1-medvedev-loses | access-date=2021-06-13}}</ref>

Nadal and [[Roger Federer]] were attempting to win a record-breaking 21st major title and become the outright leader in the men's singles major tally.<ref name="thestar.com 2021">{{cite web | title=FRENCH OPEN 2021: Federer returns; Nadal eyes Slam title 21 | website=thestar.com | date=2021-05-27 | url=https://www.thestar.com/sports/tennis/2021/05/27/french-open-2021-federer-returns-nadal-eyes-slam-title-21.html | access-date=2021-06-13}}</ref> Federer, like Djokovic, was attempting to become the first man in the Open Era to achieve the double career Grand Slam; this would be the [[2009 French Open – Men's singles|2009]] champion's last French Open appearance. This was the only time that the [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]], the three most successful men's singles [[List of Grand Slam related tennis records#Singles career totals (all time)|major champions]] of all time, were in the same half of the draw at a major.<ref>{{Cite web|last=McLeman|first=Neil|date=2021-05-28|title=French Open draw: Rafael Nadal, Roger Federer and Novak Djokovic all in the same half. together.|url=https://www.express.co.uk/sport/tennis/1442165/French-Open-draw-Rafael-Nadal-Roger-Federer-Novak-Djokovic-Roland-Garros-tennis-news|access-date=2021-05-29|website=Express.co.uk|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Rafael Nadal, Novak Djokovic And Roger Federer In Same Half Of French Open Draw {{!}} Tennis News|url=https://sports.ndtv.com/tennis/rafael-nadal-novak-djokovic-and-roger-federer-in-same-half-of-french-open-draw-2450759|access-date=2021-05-29|website=NDTVSports.com|language=en}}</ref>

For the first time in the Open Era, no French players reached the third round.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.reuters.com/lifestyle/sports/annus-horribilis-french-men-roland-garros-2021-06-01/ |title= Annus horribilis for French men at Roland Garros |date=1 June 2021|accessdate=25 January 2025}}</ref> Thiem's first-round defeat guaranteed a new French Open finalist from the bottom half of the draw; Tsitsipas ultimately emerged as that finalist. 

This marked the French Open debut of future two-time champion [[Carlos Alcaraz]], who lost in the third round to [[Jan-Lennard Struff]]. He was the youngest player to win a match at the French Open since Djokovic in [[2005 French Open – Men's singles|2005]], and the youngest to reach the third round since [[Andrei Medvedev (tennis)|Andrei Medvedev]] in [[1992 French Open – Men's singles|1992]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.firstpost.com/sports/french-open-2021-spains-carlos-alcaraz-youngest-man-to-make-roland-garros-third-round-since-1992-9683091.html|title=French Open 2021: Spain's Carlos Alcaraz youngest man to make Roland Garros third round since 1992|publisher=Firstpost|date=4 June 2021|accessdate=15 June 2021}}</ref>

==Seeded players==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on ATP rankings as of 24 May 2021. Rankings and points before are as of 31 May 2021. Because the tournament was moved a week, points of the week of 10 June 2019 include results from [[2019 MercedesCup – Singles|Stuttgart]] and [[2019 Rosmalen Grass Court Championships – Men's singles|'s-Hertogenbosch]].

As announced by the ATP in March 2021, tournaments from 4 March to 4 August 2019 points due to COVID ranking adjustments will count 50%, including their 2020 points whichever is greater. 2019 points which are higher will still be dropped as normal.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fedex-atp-rankings-adjustments-faq|title=FedEx ATP rankings COVID-19 Adjustments FAQ|website=ATP|date=3 March 2021|access-date=30 May 2021}}</ref> Accordingly, the higher of each player's 2019 or 2020 points will be replaced by his 2021 points at the end of the tournament. Note that this is a different ranking adjustment system than the one being used by the WTA for the women's event.
